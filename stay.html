Looking for a cottage or a room in Yercaud? Call us, tell us what you want, and we book a place that fits.

How it works

You tell us your dates, how many people are travelling, and your budget. We check which stays are available and suggest the ones with the views and comfort you are looking for.

Once you pick, we confirm the booking for you, so you do not need to compare many listings or call many owners.

What to tell us when you call

Check-in and check-out dates, number of adults and children, the kind of stay you prefer (family cottage, couple's room or group stay), and your approximate budget per night.

Need more than a room?

We can also arrange a cab or auto for your trip, and meals from our cloud kitchen delivered to your stay.
